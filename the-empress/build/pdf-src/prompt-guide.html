A collection of reflection and goal-setting prompts for every room of the planner — to use inside The Empress, in a notebook alongside it, or simply to sit with on their own.
Before You Begin

How to use this guide

There is no correct way through this. Take one prompt, or take ten.
This guide is not a to-do list. It's a set of doors — into your goals, your money, your body, your rest, your home — for the moments you open your planner and don't quite know where to start. Answer a prompt in The Sanctuary's reflection field, jot it in the margins, or just read it and let it sit.
  • Pick whichever room you're already standing in — there's no required order.
  • Revisit the same prompt over months; your answer changing over time is the point.
  • Skip anything that doesn't feel true today. Come back to it, or don't.
  • Use The Ledger's goal-setting prompts at the start of a new quarter for the best results.
  • None of these are graded. There's no red state here either.

Prompts

Time & money

The Court
  • What does a realistic day actually look like for me right now — not an idealised one?
  • Where am I over-scheduling out of guilt rather than need?
  • What's one recurring commitment I keep and never question?
  • What time of day do I actually have energy — and does my schedule respect it?
  • What would I remove from this week if nothing would fall apart?
The Vault
  • What does "enough" money actually mean to me this month?
  • Where does money leave my hands without my full attention?
  • What's one bill or subscription I've stopped questioning?
  • What would change if I looked at The Vault once a week, on purpose?
  • What's a financial goal I've been quietly avoiding naming?

Prompts

Body & ambition

The Body
  • What is my body asking for right now that I've been overriding?
  • What does movement feel like when it isn't a punishment?
  • What pattern have I noticed in my energy or cycle this month?
  • What's one habit I keep abandoning — and what would make it easier, not harder?
  • When did I last feel genuinely well, and what was different that day?
The Ledger

Goal-setting, start of quarter

  • What do I want to be true about my life three months from now?
  • Which of my current goals still feels like mine, and which feels inherited?
  • What's the smallest version of this goal that still counts as progress?
  • What has quietly stalled, and does it deserve a milestone or a release?
  • What would I attempt if I weren't worried about doing it perfectly?

The Sanctuary
  • What's one thing that went well today, no matter how small?
  • What is your body asking for right now?
  • What are you avoiding, and what's underneath the avoidance?
  • Where did you show up for yourself this week?
  • What would "enough" look like today?
  • What's one thing you can let go of before tomorrow?
  • Who or what gave you energy this week?
  • What does rest actually look like for you today — not what it should look like?
  • What's a small win you haven't given yourself credit for?
  • If today had a title, what would it be?
  • What boundary do you need to protect this week?
  • What are you proud of that no one else saw?

Home & closing ritual

The Estate
  • What part of my home affects my mood most, and is it tended to?
  • What chore do I avoid — and would a smaller version of it get done instead?
  • What would make meal planning feel like less of a mental load this week?
  • What's one errand I've been carrying in my head instead of on a list?
  • What does "kept," not "perfect," look like for my home this week?
Closing Ritual

A weekly return to The Throne Room

  • What actually got my attention this week — and did it deserve it?
  • What am I carrying into next week that I could set down instead?
  • What's the one thing, if it's the only thing that happens, that would make next week feel good?

Set that answer as one of your three priorities in The Throne Room, and let the rest of the week organise itself around it.
