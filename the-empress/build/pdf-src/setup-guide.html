Welcome

How The Empress works

Two layers, both always present. One is how you run your life. The other is how you support the brain doing the running.

LAYER 1 — THE EMPRESS (core)

Seven named rooms hold every part of a full life: your schedule, your money, your body, your goals, your self-care, and your home — plus a landing hub that shows you what matters today. You move through the rooms the way you'd move through a house, not the way you'd click through tabs on a spreadsheet.

The Empress plans across every horizon at once: today, this week, this month, and the long arc of your goals — all visible, none of them forced into a single rhythm.

LAYER 2 — THE COURT TOOLKIT (optional, on by default)

Seven small, independent features live underneath the core system for the days willpower alone doesn't cut it — task avoidance, energy that isn't the same every day, the need for a bit more external structure, and progress tracking that never punishes you for being human.

Every one of the seven toolkit features can be switched off individually in Settings. Nothing here is mandatory. Turn on only what actually helps.

THE NO-SHAME PRINCIPLE

There are no red states in The Empress. No punishing streak resets. No numbers designed to make you feel behind. Progress is shown in warm gold and amber tones — because a planner that shames you into using it stops getting used. This is a system built to be picked back up, gently, as many times as you need to.

Getting Started

Opening & saving your file

Your planner is a single file. Here's how it stores your data, and how to make sure you never lose it.

OPENING THE FILE

1
Double-click KiTeeStudio-TheEmpress-EDITABLE.html — it opens directly in your browser. No install, no login, no internet connection required.
2
Everything you enter is saved automatically to that browser, on that device, the moment you make a change.
3
Bookmark the file or keep a shortcut on your desktop so you can return to the exact same planner each time.

LINKING A REAL FILE ON YOUR DISK (RECOMMENDED)

In Settings, under "Your data, your file," choose Create linked file. Pick a spot on your computer — your Documents folder, or a synced folder like iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Dropbox. From then on, every change you make saves straight to that real file automatically.

Why this matters: a linked file survives clearing your browser's data, and if you save it inside a synced folder, it keeps itself backed up and available across your devices — with no account or subscription involved.

This feature works best in desktop Chrome or Edge. If your browser doesn't support it, you won't see the option — your data will still save safely inside the browser itself, and the Export option below becomes your backup method.

MANUAL BACKUPS

Whether or not you link a file, use Export backup (.json) in Settings any time you want an extra copy. Import backup restores from any exported file — useful when moving to a new computer or browser.

The Empress — Setup Guide03
The Rooms

A tour of your kingdom, part one

Each room is built for one part of your life, with its own accent colour and its own rhythm.

The Throne Room — your landing hub

The first thing you see: today's schedule, your top 1–3 priorities, a money snapshot, a body/habit pulse, and a gentle self-care nudge if you haven't checked in lately. Not everything — just what matters right now.

The Court — schedule

Day and week views of your appointments, time-blocked tasks, and recurring commitments, side by side. If Energy-Based Planning is on, tell it your current capacity and it'll surface what actually fits.

The Vault — money

What's coming in, what's going out, and what's due — income, bills, and everyday spending in one place, with a six-month chart. Multi-currency built in (GBP, USD, EUR, AUD, CAD). This is everyday budgeting, not debt payoff planning.

The Body — health

Movement and wellness habits with gentle, non-punishing streaks; a general wellness log; and a cycle tracker for period start/end dates, flow, symptoms and mood, with a simple next-cycle estimate once you've logged a couple of cycles.

The Rooms & The Toolkit

A tour of your kingdom, part two

The Ledger — goals

Quarterly, yearly, and long-term goals. Turn any goal into five auto-generated milestones (20% each) so progress moves in small, frequent wins instead of one giant leap.

The Sanctuary — self-care

Mood check-ins, rest logging, and rotating reflection prompts. Deliberately the lowest-pressure room — no streaks, no red states, ever.

The Estate — home

Chores, errands, and a weekly meal rotation. The lowest-stakes room functionally, styled with the same care as everywhere else.

The Court Toolkit — toggle in Settings
Redirect Method — a smaller adjacent step when a task feels stuck.
Micro-Milestones — goals auto-broken into 5 wins.
Visual-First Progress — bars & rings, not just numbers.
Body Doubling Mode — a visible "working alongside you" session timer.
Task Fog Log — judgment-free capture for what you can't start yet.
Energy-Based Planning — tag tasks by energy; see what fits now.
Check-In Nudges — gentle prompts, never punishing.

Care & Troubleshooting

Keeping your planner running smoothly

SWITCHING MODES

Use the toggle in the top right to move between In Gold (light) and In Shadow (dark). Both are fully designed — pick whichever suits your eyes and your hour of the day. Your choice is remembered automatically.

MY DATA LOOKS LIKE IT DISAPPEARED

Check you're opening the file in the same browser and same device you were using before — local data is tied to both. If you linked a real file, use Reconnect in Settings; browsers occasionally ask you to reconfirm permission. If you have any exported backup (.json), use Import backup to restore it.

STARTING COMPLETELY FRESH

Reset everything in Settings clears all data on this device permanently and returns you to the example starter content. Export a backup first if there's anything you want to keep.

A NOTE ON PRIVACY

The Empress makes no network requests, has no accounts, and sends nothing anywhere. Every word you write about your money, your body, or your mood stays on your device, in your file, under your control — permanently.

Welcome to your kingdom. Take what serves you, ignore what doesn't, and come back to it as many times as you need — The Empress will be exactly where you left it.
